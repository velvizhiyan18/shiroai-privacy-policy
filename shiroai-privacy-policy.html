ShiroAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

ShiroAI ("we", "us", or "our") is an Instagram messaging automation application that helps manage and respond to Instagram direct messages. This Privacy Policy explains what information ShiroAI may process, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Process

When ShiroAI is connected to an Instagram account, it may process information needed to receive and respond to Instagram messages, including:

  • Instagram-scoped user identifiers associated with people who send messages;
  • the content of Instagram direct messages and related message metadata;
  • the Instagram account identifier and information necessary to send a reply; and
  • technical information required to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information only as necessary to provide and operate the ShiroAI Instagram messaging automation, including receiving messages, generating draft replies, obtaining human approval when configured, sending approved replies, maintaining service security, and troubleshooting technical problems.

3. Human Review

Where human approval is enabled, a draft response may be sent to an authorized operator through the configured approval workflow before an Instagram reply is sent. The operator uses this information only to review and approve or reject the proposed response.

4. Third-Party Services

ShiroAI may use third-party services required to provide the automation workflow, such as Meta/Instagram for messaging and n8n or other configured infrastructure for workflow processing. Information is shared with these services only as necessary to provide the requested functionality or operate the service.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve technical issues, comply with legal obligations, or as otherwise configured by the account owner. We do not retain information longer than necessary for these purposes.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through ShiroAI. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of personal information processed by ShiroAI by contacting us at velvizhiyan2007@gmail.com. You may also disconnect the Instagram account from the application. After receiving a valid deletion request, we will delete information that we are not required to retain for legal, security, or legitimate operational purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

ShiroAI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the service, please contact us so that we can review and delete it where appropriate.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this location with a revised "Last updated" date.
